Who We Are

Two Friends
One Idea
A Shared Dream

Two friends, one idea, and a shared vision to take the goodness of South India to the world. That is where the journey of GlobServ International Traders LLP began. What started as conversations about quality, agriculture, and global opportunities gradually grew into a commitment to build something meaningful together. With determination and a belief in the value of authentic Indian produce, that idea was transformed into action, and GlobServ was born.

Rooted in the God-blessed lands of Tamil Nadu, a region celebrated for its rich agricultural heritage, fertile landscapes, and generations of farming knowledge, we carry a simple promise: to deliver quality products to global markets with honesty, consistency, and care.

We believe that every product we export represents not only our company, but also the farmers, communities, and traditions behind it. From Spirulina, Aloe Vera, and Vetiver (Khus) to Basmati Rice (1121), Chillies, Turmeric, and other agricultural products, we are committed to building lasting relationships with buyers who value quality, reliability, and transparent sourcing from India.

Our Catalog

Premium agricultural products backed by technical expertise

Core Export Tamil Nadu, India

Spirulina

Cultivated and processed spirulina, available as dried powder or flakes. Grown under controlled conditions with consistent quality control for food, nutraceutical and feed applications.

Core Export Tamil Nadu, India

Aloe Vera

Fresh aloe vera leaves and processed aloe vera powder, produced from mature Barbadensis Miller plants. Suited for cosmetics, nutraceuticals and beverage applications.

Core Export South India

Khus (Vetiver) Roots

Sun-dried Khus (Vetiver) roots, sourced from South India and valued globally for their fragrance in perfumery, essential oils, mats and traditional cooling applications.

Optional North India

Basmati 1121 Rice

Extra-long grain Pusa Basmati 1121, available as an optional addition alongside our core export range. Aged and sortex-cleaned for consistent grain length.

Optional South India

Chilli

Sun-dried whole red chilli and powdered chilli, available on request for buyers combining orders with our core products.

Optional Erode, Tamil Nadu

Turmeric

Erode turmeric — fingers and powder — known for high curcumin content. Available on request alongside our core export range.

How We Work

From enquiry to delivery.

A structured, six-step workflow used on every export order — no exceptions, regardless of destination or product.

01

Enquiry

Share your product, quantity, grade and destination port.

02

Sample & Quote

We send samples for approval and a formal FOB / CIF quote.

03

Confirmation

Order confirmed with agreed Incoterms, payment and advance.

04

Sourcing & Packing

Procurement, grading, cleaning and packing to specification.

05

QC & Documentation

Third-party QC where applicable plus full export paperwork.

06

Shipping

Dispatch by sea or air freight to your nominated port.
